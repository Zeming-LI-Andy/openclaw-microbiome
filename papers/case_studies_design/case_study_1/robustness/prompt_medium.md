You are provided with rhizosphere shotgun metagenomic data and matched 16S rRNA gene amplicon data from a tomato bacterial-wilt experiment.
Conduct a comprehensive exploratory and comparative microbiome analysis covering both 16S rRNA and shotgun metagenomic profiling.

## Data characterization

- Describe the dataset structure and assess quality.
- Visualize experimental design, sequencing depth, and QC, stratified by cultivar, timepoint, and treatment.

## 16S diversity, taxonomy, and differential abundance

- Compare alpha and beta diversity among cultivars and between resistant and susceptible groups, stratified by timepoint.
- Profile taxonomic composition.
- At each timepoint, identify taxa that differ between each resistant focal cultivar and the susceptible control.
- Identify markers enriched in both **HG64** and **HF12** at **T1**, **T2C**, and **T2**.

## Shotgun taxonomy and differential abundance

- Profile species-level composition.
- At each timepoint, identify species that differ between each resistant focal cultivar and the susceptible control.
- Identify markers enriched in both **HG64** and **HF12** at **T1**, **T2C**, and **T2**.
